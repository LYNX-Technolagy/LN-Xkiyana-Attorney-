Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

LN Xokiyana Attorneys ("we", "us", "the firm") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 ("POPIA").

1. Information we collect

We collect personal information you provide directly to us, including when you complete our contact form, call our offices, email us, or engage us to represent you. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, and telephone number
  • The nature of your legal matter, as described by you
  • Any documents or correspondence you send to us
  • Billing and payment information, where applicable

When you visit our website, we may also automatically collect limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. This information is aggregated and used to keep the website secure and functioning correctly.

2. How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide legal advice
  • Represent you in legal proceedings and negotiations
  • Comply with our legal, regulatory, and professional obligations
  • Maintain accurate client records and billing
  • Improve the quality and security of our services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use your information for marketing purposes unless you have specifically consented to receive communications from us.

3. Legal basis for processing

Under POPIA, we process your personal information on one or more of the following bases:

  • Contract — to perform our engagement with you
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law and professional rules
  • Legitimate interest — to operate and protect our practice
  • Consent — where you have expressly given it, for example for marketing

4. Sharing your information

We may share your information with third parties only where necessary to deliver our services or comply with the law. This may include:

  • Counsel, correspondent attorneys, and experts engaged on your matter
  • Courts, the Road Accident Fund, and other statutory bodies
  • Our professional indemnity insurers and auditors
  • Service providers who host or support our systems, under confidentiality obligations

Where we share information with third parties, we require them to protect it to at least the standard set out in this policy.

5. How long we keep it

We retain client files for the period required by the Legal Practice Council and applicable professional rules — generally at least five years after the conclusion of a matter, and longer where required by law or where a claim may still arise. Once the retention period ends, we securely destroy or de-identify the information.

6. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against loss, unauthorised access, and misuse. These include access controls on our systems, encrypted communication, secure storage of physical files, and confidentiality obligations on our staff and service providers. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we act promptly on any incident that may affect your information.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, or no longer needed
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer using the details at the end of this policy.

8. Cookies

Our website uses only the cookies strictly necessary for it to function. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. If you access the site through a link from a third-party service, that service may set its own cookies, which are governed by its own privacy policy.

9. Children

Our services are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18, except where a child is a party to a matter on which we are instructed and a parent or legal guardian has provided consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated to active clients directly.
